The Western Garment Fabric Studio

MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE

At Fabreen manufacturing is a passion. This passion has driven us to set up world-class manufacturing facilities with extreme operational efficiencies. Over the years, we have earned an enviable reputation for flawless development of new extraordinary fabrics.

Manufacturing divisions of Fabreen not only create the jobs for skilled workforce, but also train unskilled workers, helping create a strong talent pool. Every product we create with the ‘No Error’ tag is a source of great honor and pride.

VISION & MISSION

To be a global brand in textile

with maintaining international standards in production with modern infrastructure, advanced applications and cutting edge technology.

Fabreen aims to be world class fashion organization producing diverse range of products for the global fashion market.

We seek to achieve customer delight through excellence in manufacturing and customer service, based on creative combination of state-of-the-art technology and human resources.

Product Innovation

Our expertise in developing products and markets from Idea to fruition' and beyond. Our constant focus on innovation has helped us to emerge as a trendsetter in various markets and be known in textile for our unbeatable range of products.

Our operations span from the exploration & production of yarn to the manufacture of Grey Fabrics, Garment Fabrics, Dyed Fabrics, Fancy Print Sarees & Dress Material.
