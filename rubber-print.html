About Our Designing Rubber Print Services

Rubber Print is a bold and durable textile printing technique that creates a raised, matte finish on fabric. It is widely used in fashion and garment printing to achieve strong colors, sharp designs, and long-lasting print quality.

Rubber printing is a specialized fabric printing process where a thick, rubber-based ink is applied to the fabric surface to create a solid, opaque, and slightly raised design. This technique is known for its smooth matte finish and excellent color coverage, making it ideal for bold graphics, logos, and statement patterns.

Our Design Expertise

  • Thick, opaque print with matte finish
  • Slightly raised texture for strong visual impact
  • High color intensity and coverage
  • Excellent adhesion to fabric surface
  • Suitable for bold and graphic designs

Fully Trained Employees

Unlike regular pigment printing, rubber print sits prominently on the fabric surface, giving the design a distinct texture and depth. It allows for high-impact visuals that remain vibrant and intact even after repeated washing and regular wear. Due to its strong adhesion and flexibility, rubber print maintains its shape without cracking when handled correctly.

Rubber printing is commonly used in contemporary fashion, casual wear, and streetwear collections where durability and visual strength are essential. It supports both simple and complex designs and works well on various fabric types when applied with proper technique and finishing.
